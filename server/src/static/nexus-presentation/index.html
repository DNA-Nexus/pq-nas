Your own cloud, without giving away your life.

DNA-Nexus is a self-hosted NAS platform for files, photos, media, sharing and private social features. It gives your own storage a modern cloud-like experience while keeping your data under your control.

Built around ownership

DNA-Nexus is designed for people, families, teams and communities who want modern sharing without handing photos, documents and private moments to advertising platforms.

01

Data stays close

Your files and media live on storage you control. The server provides access, apps and sharing around that storage.

02

Apps feel connected

Files, galleries, sharing, workspaces and Circle Stack are designed as one product, not random disconnected tools.

03

Privacy is the point

No ad-driven feed. No profiling-first business model. DNA-Nexus is built around user control and clear sharing.

Why it matters

Your NAS is no longer an island.

DNA-Nexus starts with practical NAS features, then grows into collaboration, media publishing and federation. Instead of uploading everything into someone else’s platform, your NAS becomes the place where your digital life begins.

  • Browse and manage files from a clean web interface.
  • Share albums, videos and uploads without moving everything to big cloud platforms.
  • Connect with other DNA-Nexus users through Circle Stack and future Nodus federation.
