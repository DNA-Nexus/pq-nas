Highlights

Best features, explained simply.

DNA-Nexus should present itself as a friendly private cloud, not as a complicated server project. These are the feature messages worth showing early.

File versioning

Accidents become history, not disaster.

Accidentally overwrote a file? DNA-Nexus gives you options instead of panic. Advanced file versioning lets you keep multiple versions of important files.

  • Tag important file versions so you know what changed.
  • Download older copies or restore previous work.
  • Compare text-file versions side by side.
WS

External workspaces

Work together without giving away your whole NAS. Open selected workspaces to external DNA Connect friends. Let trusted people read, edit, upload and download files together with you.

TD

Trusted devices

QR-based mobile pairing and trusted-device flows help make access feel secure and modern without forcing everything through third-party identity platforms.

AC

Activity and transparency

Users and admins can see recent activity. Uploads, deleted files, shares and future security events can become understandable.

SH

Clear sharing

Share files, albums, videos or upload links explicitly. DNA-Nexus is built around understandable access, not hidden platform rules.

Photo Gallery

Your photo library, connected to your own storage.

Browse your photos in a beautiful gallery, explore where they were taken on a map, and compare images side by side with synchronized zoom to inspect even the smallest details.

  • See camera, lens, lighting and metadata statistics.
  • Quick-edit metadata and search with ease.
  • Create albums, share them with friends, or publish them into Circle Stack.
