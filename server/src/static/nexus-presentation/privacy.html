Trust

A cloud service built around clear control.

DNA-Nexus is designed for users and organizations that want practical cloud features, transparent sharing and a service relationship they can understand.

Cloud convenience without unnecessary complexity.

The service brings together storage, sharing, media, customer uploads and administration so users can work from one place instead of many disconnected tools.

  • Sharing is explicit and manageable.
  • Business upload pages can be branded and purpose-specific.
  • Media, links and collaboration tools are part of the same service.
  • Future server federation can connect selected communities and organizations.
Future direction

From cloud storage to connected cloud communities.

The long-term direction includes server-to-server federation. When users from different DNA-Nexus services connect, their servers can discover each other through Nodus and exchange selected Circle Stack activity.

  • Useful for families, clubs, associations and local communities.
  • Selected activity can be shared without turning everything into one central social platform.
  • The service can grow from storage into a broader digital community platform.

Who is it for?

Homes, families, small businesses, schools, clubs, associations, communities and privacy-conscious organizations.
