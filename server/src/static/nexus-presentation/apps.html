App suite

One NAS, many useful apps.

The first presentation should show the app family without drowning visitors in technical details. DNA-Nexus feels like one private cloud platform, not a folder full of unrelated tools.

FM

File Manager

Browse, upload, organize, preview, edit text files and manage shared workspace content.

PG

Photo Gallery

Enjoy photo collections, metadata, map views, comparisons and album sharing from your own storage.

RS

Reel Stack

A video gallery for stored movies and clips, with thumbnails, metadata and search direction.

NW

Neonwave

A personal music library for browsing, playing and organizing audio stored on the NAS.

ES

Echo Stack

A private link vault and read-it-later archive for saving important pages and references.

DZ

Drop Zone

One-way public upload links for receiving files from outside users without giving full account access.

CS

Circle Stack

A privacy-first social layer for posts, album shares, reactions and Memory Node posts.

Circle Stack

A social feed where your media stays yours.

Connect with other DNA-Nexus NAS users through Circle Stack. Share posts, photos, albums and moments while keeping the original data in your own storage.

  • No ad-driven feed and no profiling-first business model.
  • Post albums from Photo Gallery without surrendering the original media.
  • Create Memory Node posts and invite friends to add their own moments, photos or comments around the same event.
